# Governance

Owner and primary maintainer: [Maged Taha](https://github.com/magedtahaaa), as confirmed by the owner during publication preparation.

The maintainer reviews changes, records decisions, triages issues and manages releases. Significant proposals should describe the problem, alternatives, compatibility/privacy implications and acceptance criteria. Additional maintainers may be appointed through explicit agreement and documented responsibilities.

Client-specific data, copy and assets are outside the public project's scope. Only synthetic fixtures may be used in examples and tests. Preserve third-party attribution. Do not mark roadmap items delivered without validation.

Original project work uses the MIT License. GitHub private vulnerability reporting is enabled. A dedicated private conduct/escalation contact and the first tagged release remain pending. No support SLA is offered.
